## 🔥 Featured Projects

### 🏠 Servandi — AI Front Desk for Home-Service Businesses
An AI system that makes sure HVAC, plumbing, and electrical companies never lose a job to a missed call. It instantly texts back missed calls, answers and qualifies leads in seconds, books them on the calendar, and requests 5-star reviews, running 24/7 with no extra staff.

🔗 **Live demo:** [servandi.com](https://servandi.com) &nbsp;·&nbsp; `LLMs` `Twilio` `n8n` `Automation` `CRM`
- Recovers revenue lost to missed calls and slow replies
- Instant lead response, day or night, no extra staff
- Automated booking and 5-star review generation

### 🤖 Private Multimodal AI Assistant
A fully local AI assistant that handles both text and images (captioning and generation) with **zero external APIs**, so all data stays private and there are no per-message costs. Orchestrated with n8n and PostgreSQL for persistent memory.

`LLaMA` `Gemma` `ComfyUI` `n8n` `PostgreSQL`
- Complete data privacy, no third-party APIs
- Multimodal: understands and generates text and images

### ⚙️ Business Reporting Automation
Replaced a manual reporting process that took 6 hours with an automated Python and SQL pipeline feeding real-time Power BI dashboards, cutting the work down to seconds.

`Python` `SQL` `Power BI`
- 6 hours of manual work reduced to seconds
- Real-time dashboards for faster decisions

### 🗣️ Multilingual Audio AI
Identifies 7 languages from audio (Arabic, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish) in real time, delivered as both web and mobile applications.

`Python` `Flask` `Deep Learning`
- Real-time, high-accuracy language detection
